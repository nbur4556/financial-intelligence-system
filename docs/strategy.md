# Financial Intelligence Strategy

## Objective
Transition from traditional "budgeting" to a business-style "Financial Intelligence" (FI) system focused on analytical reporting and informed decision-making rather than restrictive spending limits.

## 1. Chart of Accounts (Data Categorization)
Money will be tracked in broad, actionable categories:
- **Fixed Costs (OpEx):** The "cost of existing" (Rent/Mortgage, Utilities, Insurance, Subscriptions).
- **Variable Costs (Growth/Life):** "Quality of life" levers (Groceries, Dining, Entertainment, Travel).
- **Capital Allocations (Investments):** The "wealth building" engine (Brokerage, Retirement, HYSA).
- **Debt Service:** Liability reduction (Credit card interest, Student loans).

## 2. Data Pipeline (Collection)
- **Method:** Extract transaction data from banks/credit cards (via CSV exports or aggregators).
- **Processing:** Map raw transaction descriptions to the Chart of Accounts.

## 3. Reporting Suite (Analysis)
Generate Monthly and Quarterly reports focusing on Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
- **Burn Rate:** Average monthly spending (Fixed + Variable).
- **Savings Rate:** `(Income - Expenses) / Income`.
- **Efficiency Ratio:** `Fixed Costs / Total Income`.
- **Variance:** Month-over-month comparison against a 3-month rolling average.

## 4. Feedback Loop (Decision Making)
- **Monthly Review:** Tactical adjustments to variable spending and anomaly detection.
- **Quarterly Review:** Strategic shifts in capital allocation and savings trends.
- **Annual Review:** Baseline adjustments for fixed costs and long-term goal alignment.
